05FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still have one? Call 1-833-624-3863, Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm EST.

01Will applying affect my credit score?

No. We may perform a soft credit inquiry during the review process, which does not affect your credit score.

02What do I need to apply?

The last 3 to 6 months of business bank statements, a valid government-issued ID, and basic information about your business — your EIN, business address and monthly revenue.

03Do I need tax returns or financial statements?

For most products, no tax returns or financial statements are required.

04How fast can I get funded?

Most applications are reviewed and approved within 4 to 8 hours, and in many cases you can go from application to funding in 24 to 48 hours.

05Who qualifies?

Most products require at least 1 year in business, $10,000 or more in monthly revenue and an active U.S. business bank account. Revenue-based financing may be available from 4 months in business and $7,500 or more in monthly revenue.

06How does repayment work on a merchant cash advance?

Repayment is a small fixed percentage of your daily revenue that is automatically applied toward the balance.

07Does Monera provide every product directly?

Monera Capital provides merchant cash advances directly. Business loans, business lines of credit, equipment financing, SBA loans and accounts receivable factoring are offered through our third-party partners.
